# Autonomous Execution Guidelines

1. **Do Not Block on Plan Approval:**
   - Execute user requests proactively and directly.
   - Do not ask for user permission or pause for approval before executing implementation plans.
   - When a plan or redesign is needed, propose and execute it immediately.

2. **Design & Aesthetic Principles for AIKO:**
   - No generic AI clichés: Avoid neon glowing purple, pink, cyan gradients, stars, sparkles, and robot/brain motifs.
   - Strict Zero-Emoji policy: Never use emojis anywhere in the UI, button labels, system messages, or notifications.
   - Maintain the bespoke Industrial Workstation design language: Obsidian charcoal, brushed titanium, and warm bronze/amber accents.
